# Prima, round 2: the intermediate player learning pieces

*Reviewed 12 November in the preview host (Studio look, the tablet's 1280 × 800 landscape and 800 × 1280 portrait), with the
simulated piano. Every finding says whether I **saw** it in the preview, **inferred** it from the code, or it can only be
confirmed **on the tablet**.*

## 1. In short

I was the owner's own seat: hands together at an early-intermediate level, 30 minutes a day, two Library pieces to bring up
to tempo. I took Petzold's **Minuet in G** (BWV Anh. 114) as the main piece and Tchaikovsky's **Album for the Young No. 15**
as the second. I read them through in Wait for me, found the hard bars, looped bars 25–28 ten times getting better each pass,
went hands apart and then together, brought the tempo from 60 to 66, played the Minuet through in Play, and used Today,
Sight-read and the scales as a warm-up.

**What's already right.** The round-1 complaint is fixed: after a run or a pass, how it went is on the screen with the
music, no scrolling. Round a loop going straight on, the bar says *Pass 5 · Last pass 94% ↑* while you play, and when you
stop, the summary names every pass ("4 passes… 77%, 88%, 96% and 96%"). The look back draws each stretch as a line from first
to last with the tempo, which is exactly "you played this part N times and you improved this much". Play is clean: the page,
a slim strip, a one-line verdict at the end. Portrait is the best layout of all.

**The three things that most need changing:**

1. **The look back and the in-depth review disagree about which bar is hard, and "Still hard" doesn't mean still.** After ten
   passes that went 77% → 100%, the look back said *Still hard: bar 25, something to fix 10 of the 10 times* (one note let
   go early, even on the perfect pass), while the in-depth review said *Look at bar 27: 22 wrong*. The review then outlined
   bar 27 with all-green notes, because it shows the latest pass, which was clean. Three screens, three answers.
2. **"Wait after each pass" throws the loop away.** Set a loop to wait (Hands-on's default, and the only way to look between
   passes), and each pass becomes a lone run: the summary says *One pass of bars 25 to 28* ten times, the bar loses its pass
   count and its ↑/↓, and About counts 10 "runs". The improvement only shows up when you finish the session.
3. **The loop's own bars fall off the screen after a pass in landscape.** The summary takes the bottom half; the music keeps
   the line it was scrolled to, so with a loop on bars 25–28 you see bars 16–23 and the top sliver of the loop (picture 1).
   The marks you most want are hidden under the summary.

Also worth doing soon: a way from "look at bar 15" (Play's verdict, the look back's Still hard) straight to *practise bar 15*,
and something on the piece's front that says where you left off.

## 2. My month

The clock can't be moved in the preview, so my "month" is the steps a month would have, played in order on one day from
`seed/today` (six weeks of history, today half done). That means I never saw **Since yesterday**: the only comparisons I could
get were **Earlier today**.

**Week 1: choosing and reading.** Library › **Near my level** (my reading level is 4, so it lists pieces reading mostly at
4–6): 21 pieces. It's a good shortlist, but with clutter: two "Aria" entries, three "Menuet in G" variants and a "Sample tune
- abcm2ps (voices.abc) - Praeludium II" (saw). I opened the Minuet: a calm front with Practice and Play (saw). **Practice**,
Wait for me, the whole piece: 274 steps answered, one wrong key in six. The run ended with the music marked (wrong keys in
red with both note names), and under it *Right first time 364 of 408 · Wrong keys 44 · Notes a minute 710* (saw). Nothing
named the hard bars: the Wait for me summary has no **Look at** line, and **Finish practice** said *Still hard: No bar went
wrong more than once*, because one run can't be "more than once". From a first read-through, I had to find the hard bars by
eye on the red marks.

**Week 2: loops.** I dragged along the strip for bars 25–28, set the tempo to 60, and in Options set **Round a loop: Wait after
each pass** so I could look between passes. Ten passes, each better (`/keys/perform`, right 0.70 → 0.95, spread 80 → 25 ms,
fewer notes let go early). After each pass: the bar's headline (77%, 77%, 77%… 85%, 92%, 96%, 100%) and the summary under the
music, which each time began *One pass of bars 25 to 28* and went on about only that pass (saw). **Finish practice**: the look
back's line went 77% → 100%, *best 100%*, tempo 60, and Still hard *bar 25, 10 of 10* (picture 2). **In-depth review**: 83%
overall, 217/260, *Look at bar 27*, with bars 27, 28, 26, 25 as four identical red cells and bar 27's notes all green
(picture 3).

**Week 3: hands apart, hands together, tempo up.** Same loop, **Go straight on**, at 66: right hand (four passes, 88 → 94%),
left hand (80 → 90%), both (77 → 96%), with the notes arriving live. While playing, the bar showed *Bar 27 · Pass 3 · Last
pass 88% ↑*, and after Stop the summary listed every pass (saw). That's the loop review I wanted. The look back had four
stretches, each its own line, and a later session on the loop said *Earlier today: Bars 25–28 at 66, from 60* (saw).

**Week 4: playing it through.** **Play**: the page, *Minuet in G Major · Play · Tempo 66 · Start · Done*. Stopped at bar 12:
*90% right in time · 62 of 69 notes · look at bar 1 · stopped at bar 12*. Again, the whole piece: *92% right in time · 374 of
408 notes · look at bar 15*, every note marked, Again / In-depth review / Done (saw, picture 4). About now says *Played through
once In time: 92% at 66*. The second piece, the Tchaikovsky, I practised the **Hands-on** way: each run's review took the
screen, 78% → 86% → 96% over three runs, each review on its own (saw).

**Warm-up, most days.** Today plans **20 minutes**: technique 4, notes 3, rhythm 3, pause 1, sight-reading 7, one spot 2
(saw). Sight-read: 30 seconds to look, a bar of count-in, five bars, a clear summary; about a minute a passage (saw). The
scales drill opened on D minor harmonic with its fingering, five runs, Wait for me first (saw). Do they earn their minutes?
Sight-reading yes: it's the one thing that makes the next piece faster to learn. Scales, a few minutes, yes. But all 20
minutes of Today out of my 30 leaves 10 for the piece I actually sat down for, and Today never mentions it. On piece days I'd
want a 10-minute warm-up (one scale in the piece's key, three sight-reading passages) and the rest on the piece (P5).

## 3. What works (leave it alone)

- **The summary beside the music.** Standard way: after a run the music shrinks and the summary sits below it, Loop / Play
  from bar N / Show details at hand. In portrait it's ideal: the loop, its marks and the summary on one screen (saw).
- **The pass figure in the bar** while a loop goes straight on, with ↑/↓, and the **list of every pass** in the summary when
  you stop (saw).
- **The look back's lines**: one per stretch, first → last, best named, tempo as a step. Readable at a glance (saw).
- **Play**: nothing on the page while you play, a one-line verdict after, every note marked. It is the "just the sheet music"
  the owner asked for (saw).
- **The pause cover**: leaving a piece mid-session and coming back shows *Still practising? 0:47 playing and 0:20 idle so
  far* (saw).
- **Wrong notes named on the music** (*G3 G♯3*) and slips greyed: you know exactly what your hand did (saw).
- **Speed** in the preview: a piece opens in 0.4 s, Finish practice → look back in 33 ms, ten passes with no lag. The Library's
  first opening still takes about 12–14 s to store the collection (saw; the tablet will be slower).

## 4. Problems, most painful first

### P1. Three screens, three "hard bars" — bug (logic), sure

*What happened.* After ten passes of bars 25–28 that went 77% → 100%:
- look back: *Still hard: Bar 25: something to fix 10 of the 10 times it was played, mostly notes let go early*;
- in-depth review: *Look at: Bar 27 had the most to fix: 22 wrong · 6 let go early*;
- the in-depth review's outlined bar 27 shows only green notes.

*Why* (inferred from `SessionReview.cs` and `DeepReview.razor`): Still hard counts the passes in which a bar had *anything*
at all (one note let go early counts as much as three wrong notes), over the whole session, so a bar with a tiny flaw every
time beats the bar that was a mess and got fixed. The review's Look at uses weight (wrong ×3). The bar's music shows the
*latest* run's marks, which for a fixed bar are clean. And "Still" looks at the whole session, not the end of it.

*See it.* `seed/today`, Minuet in G (`library/pdmx-QmUq8P8…`), Practice, In time, tempo 60, loop 25–28, Options › Wait after
each pass, ten passes with `/keys/perform?right=0.70…0.95&spread=80…25&early=0.2…0.065`, Finish practice, In-depth review.

*Change.* One rule everywhere: weight (as the review has it), and **Still hard** only for a bar that still had trouble in the
last third of its passes; a bar that was bad and got fixed gets its own line, *Fixed: bar 27, 4 wrong on the first pass, none
on the last three*. In the review, show the bar's **worst** pass's marks beside the latest (the run's keys are kept, and so is
each pass's trouble, so the counts exist now; the worst pass's *marks* would need keeping, see the appendix).

### P2. Waiting after each pass turns a loop into ten lonely runs — friction, sure

*What happened.* With **Round a loop: Wait after each pass**, each pass is a run: the summary always says *One pass of bars
25 to 28*, the bar shows only the headline (no *Pass 7*, no ↑/↓, no "best so far"), and About says *14 runs on 1 day* for one
loop session. Hands-on's per-pass review does the same: 78%, 86%, 96% on three separate screens with no word of the one
before (saw).

*Change.* A waited loop is still a loop: keep the pass number and the trend. The first line of the summary should be the
passes so far, as going-straight-on already does: *Pass 7 · 92% ↑ · best 96% (pass 6) · 77 → 92 since pass 1*, with a tiny
row of dots. In Hands-on, put the same line at the top of the run's review. About should count a waited loop as one loop
with its passes.

### P3. The loop scrolls out of sight under the summary in landscape — bug (layout), sure, landscape only

*What happened.* At 1280 × 800, after a pass the summary takes the lower half and the music keeps its scroll: line 16–23 at
the top, the loop's bars 25–28 cut off below with only their top showing (picture 1). In portrait the loop's line is in full
view (saw).

*Change.* After a run, scroll the music so the run's bars (the loop, or the bar Look at names) are in view. In landscape a
four-bar loop fits in one line: show that line, centred.

### P4. "Look at bar N" leads nowhere from Play and the look back — gap, sure

Play's verdict says *look at bar 15*, the look back says *Still hard: bar 25*: neither is a key (saw). The Standard summary
already has *Play from bar 27* and *Loop bars 25 to 28*; these two screens should too: **Practise bar 15** starts a session
with a two-bar loop around it, at 80% of the tempo, Wait for me or In time as you last had it.

### P5. Nothing says where you left off, or what's next — gap, sure

The piece reopens with the loop and tempo you had (good), and About's first line is the last run (*Last played today: In time
at 66, 374 of 408 right*). But the last *session* isn't summarised anywhere on the front, and Library's Working on shows the
last run's figure, which after a Play is the whole piece at 92% and says nothing about the loop at 66 you were building (saw).
Today has no step for your piece: its 20 minutes are drills and sight-reading, and "One spot" is the sight-reading bar (saw).
With 30 minutes a day, I want Today to say *Minuet: you were on bars 25–28 at 66 (96% last pass). Pick up there →*.

### P6. One read-through gives no hard bars — gap, sure

After the first Wait for me run of the whole piece, the summary has no **Look at** line and the look back says *No bar went
wrong more than once* (saw). On the first day this is exactly what I need: *the bars with most to fix: 26, 27, 20*, as keys to
loop. The trouble per bar is already kept for that run.

### P7. The in-depth review's bar cells are all one red, and it lays the music out afresh — friction, fairly sure

For the loop session the four bars 27, 28, 26, 25 had very different trouble (22 wrong against 1 let go early) but all four
cells are the same full red (picture 3, saw; the shading seems to saturate early). Its headline *83% right in time* is the
whole session's average, which hides the 77 → 100 that happened. And it draws the music at a different width, so bar 27 is on
a different line than on the practice screen (saw): one more re-orientation.

*Change.* Shade by each bar's share of the worst bar; lead with first → last, not the average.

### P8. Play shows two sets of keys at the end — friction, sure

After a play-through the top strip still has **Start** and **Done**, and the bottom verdict has **Again** and **Done** (picture
4, saw). Start and Again do the same thing; two Dones. Hide the strip's keys once the verdict is up, or the verdict's.

### P9. Wait for me's summary says nothing about where — friction, sure

*Right first time 364 of 408 · Wrong keys 44 · Notes a minute 710* (saw). The figure "notes a minute" is meaningless for a
learner; the bars with the wrong keys are what I need (as P6).

### P10. Library clutter at my level — friction, sure

Near my level lists *Aria* and *Aria - repetitions*, three Menuets in G (one is "Minuet in G Major", Petzold, captioned BWV
114), a "Sample tune - abcm2ps (voices.abc) - Praeludium II", and "Op 82" with no name (saw). Tidy the titles in the bundle.

### P11. An empty practice session gets a look back — friction, sure

Tap Practice by mistake, then Finish practice: a look back with *0 stretches… Nothing was played… Still hard: No bar went
wrong more than once*, and the empty session is kept (saw). With nothing played, Finish should just go back to the front.

### P12. The session's clock is drawn twice a second, and kept twice as often — hitch risk, inferred, confirm on the tablet

`Piece.razor` renders `PracticeStrip` both in the header (landscape) and in the bar's title row (portrait); one is hidden with
CSS but both are live (in landscape I counted two `.practice-strip` elements, one `display: none`). Each has its own one-second
timer that advances the clock, redraws and calls `Ticked`, so the session is saved every 15 seconds instead of 30 and two
components redraw every second. Harmless here; on the tablet's WebView it's needless work during a run. Render one.

### P13. A page that's left behind can overwrite a finished session — preview only, sure in the preview

In the preview a browser page that's closed keeps its Blazor circuit for a few minutes; when it finally goes, its
`DisposeAsync` saves the session as it held it, *unfinished*, over the finish another page had saved. My first two sessions
came back as "paused, still going" and later runs joined them. On the tablet only one page exists at a time, so this
shouldn't happen there (inferred), but `KeepSession` writes the whole row from memory: a save that never clears `Finished`
once set would make it safe.

## 5. How I'd arrange it

The pieces are all there; the arrangement just needs to carry one thread through a session: *what am I working on, and is
it getting better?*

```
Today ──► "Your pieces" card (new, top of Today)
            Minuet in G · bars 25–28 at 66 · last pass 96% · Pick up →
            Album No. 15 · read through once · hard: 3, 31, 36 · Pick up →
          then the warm-up (Today's plan, shortened to ~10 min when a piece is on)

Piece front ── "Last session: 18 min · bars 25–28 60 → 66 · 77% → 96%"  (new line, from the session row + its runs)
   │           Practice  ·  Play
   ▼
Practice (as built)
   run / pass ─► bar: Pass 7 · 92% ↑ · best 96%       ← every pass, waiting or not
              ─► summary under the music, scrolled so the loop shows
                    line 1: the passes so far (dots), first → now
                    line 2: Look at bar N (weight rule) · [Loop it] [Slower]
   Finish practice ─► Look back
                    each stretch's line (as now)
                    Fixed: bar 27 (22 wrong → 0)          ← new
                    Still hard: bar 25, in the last 3 passes  ← recalculated
                    Since last time: bars 25–28 60 → 66, best 100 → 96
                    [Practise bar 25 next time]  ← sets the piece to open there
                    Keep practising · In-depth review · Done

Play ─► verdict: 92% · look at bar 15 [Practise bar 15 →]  · Again · Done
```

**What each pass shows, what waits for the look back, what goes:**

| Detail | After every pass | Only in the look back | Drop |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pass number, this pass's figure, ↑/↓ on the one before | ✔ (in the bar) | | |
| Best so far, first → now | ✔ (one line) | ✔ | |
| Look at bar N, with Loop it | ✔ | | |
| Notes wrong, where | ✔ (one line) | | |
| Timing / held / loud and soft / touch lines | under Show details | | |
| "One pass of bars 25 to 28" | | | ✔ (say pass 7) |
| The line chart over passes, tempo steps | | ✔ | |
| Fixed and Still hard bars | | ✔ | |
| Since last session | | ✔ | |
| Playing / idle time | the clocks | ✔ | |
| Session average "right in time" | | | ✔ (hides the progress) |
| Notes a minute (Wait for me) | | | ✔ |

**Play against Practice.** Keep both; Play is the test, Practice the work. Play's runs should appear in the next look back's
*Since last time* (a play-through is the honest measure of a week's loops), and its verdict's bar should open Practice there.

**The instructor, when it comes back.** Everything it needs to *report* is kept today: per bar trouble per pass, tempo per
run, sessions. A first instructor needs no Claude: *"Bars 25–28: 96% at 66 three sessions running → try 72"*, *"Bar 15 went
wrong in both play-throughs → loop 14–16 at 60"*, as one line at the top of the look back and on Today's piece card. Claude
could later turn a week of look backs into a paragraph.

**Joining sections.** Nothing helps with it now: I'd add, on a loop the look back calls solid (three passes ≥ 95%), a key
**Join with the next 4 bars**, which widens the loop to 25–32 and drops the tempo a step.

## 6. The five changes I'd make first

1. **One "hard bar" rule** (weight, recent passes), **Fixed** beside **Still hard**, and the review showing the bar's worst
   pass (P1).
2. **A waited loop keeps its pass number and trend** in the bar and the summary, and Hands-on's review leads with it (P2).
3. **Scroll the run's bars into view** after a run in landscape (P3).
4. **"Practise bar N →"** on Play's verdict and the look back's Still hard (P4), and the hard bars named after one read-through
   (P6).
5. **Where you left off** on the piece's front and a **Your pieces** card on Today (P5).

## Appendix: what's kept, and what could be shown from it

From `PrimaStore.Practice.cs`, `Piece.razor` (`Keep`) and `SessionReview.cs` (inferred, read):

- **Each run** (`Attempt`, kind `piece`): when, the piece, In time or Wait for me, the tempo, the level, the **keys played with
  their times** (ms from the first), and in `Measures`: the figures (right, wrong, missed, extra, held, let go early, right in
  time), the start bar, the loop's last bar, **each pass** (right, notes, right in time, and what went wrong in each bar), stopped
  and where, the hand, the session or that it was Play's, **what went wrong in each bar** of the run, and the **marks** it left
  (of the last pass, round a loop).
- **Each session** (`PracticeSession`): the piece, started, finished, playing and idle seconds, paused, last played, the way.

**Could be shown now, with nothing new kept:** a pass's number and trend in a waited loop (the runs are consecutive in one
session with the same bars); "Fixed" bars (per-pass trouble); the last session on the front and on Today (session row + its
runs); a bar's history over days (every run's per-bar trouble); the fastest tempo a stretch has gone clean; how many times a
bar was played in all (each run's from/to and pass count); per-pass timing spread (the keys and their times are kept for the
whole run, passes included, so it can be worked out again).

**Would need keeping anew:** each pass's **time** (all passes share the run's time now, so "best, the later" can't tell them
apart); each pass's **marks** (only the last pass's are kept, so the worst pass of bar 27 can't be shown); a **target tempo**
for a piece; a **"next time" note** or chosen spot from the look back; a waited loop's passes **grouped** as one loop (or
worked out from consecutive runs with the same bars).
